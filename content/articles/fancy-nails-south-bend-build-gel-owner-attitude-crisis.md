A relaxing salon visit can quickly become frustrating when technical mistakes are followed by poor communication. According to one customer review involving **Fancy Nails in South Bend**, an enjoyable pedicure was overshadowed by a problematic Builder Gel manicure and an interaction with salon management that left the client feeling dismissed rather than supported.

## A Builder Gel Service Gone Wrong

The reviewer said she requested a Builder Gel fill to maintain years of healthy natural nail growth. Instead, she reported that the finished structure appeared uneven, with several nails visibly leaning to one side.

Builder Gel requires careful shaping because the product serves as the structural foundation for the natural nail. When that structure is uneven, the appearance and long-term durability of the manicure may both suffer.

For the client, the disappointment extended beyond appearance. She believed years of patiently growing strong natural nails had been compromised during a single appointment.

## An Owner's Response That Deepened the Frustration

After the first technician struggled, a second technician identified by the reviewer as the salon owner reportedly took over.

Rather than calming the situation, the customer said the interaction became increasingly uncomfortable. The review quoted the owner as saying, "I'm not about to keep trying colors on you," after the client had compared only two pink polish options.

## A Patch Instead of a Proper Correction

According to the review, the salon attempted to correct the uneven Builder Gel without removing and rebuilding the structure.

The customer felt the repair merely covered the existing problem instead of resolving it. She also included photographs with the review, stating they accurately reflected the uneven nail shape and inconsistent polish application she received.

## Why Recovery Matters

Even experienced technicians occasionally encounter services that require correction. Honest communication, patience during color selection, and a willingness to rebuild work when necessary frequently leave a stronger impression than attempting a quick cosmetic fix.

## Editorial Perspective

In beauty services, technical skill and customer experience are closely connected. Clients investing time to grow healthy natural nails expect Builder Gel services to protect that progress, while respectful communication becomes especially important whenever a correction is needed.

The review involving Fancy Nails in South Bend illustrates how an unsuccessful repair and an interaction perceived as dismissive can overshadow an otherwise positive visit. For salons, careful workmanship, transparency, and professionalism remain among the most effective ways to earn lasting trust.
