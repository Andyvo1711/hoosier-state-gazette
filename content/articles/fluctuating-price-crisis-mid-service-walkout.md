# The "Fluctuating Price" Crisis and the Mid-Service Walkout: When Price Gouging Ruins a Birthday

**Customer Service Bulletin – Pricing Transparency & Customer Trust Feature**

According to a customer review involving **Q Nails Salon**, what was intended to be a birthday self-care appointment turned into a frustrating experience centered on changing price quotes and declining confidence in the service. The customer said the uncertainty over pricing ultimately led her to leave before the appointment was completed.

## Multiple Price Changes

The customer explained that she requested a straightforward **acrylic fill** and no additional services.

According to her account, the initial quote was **$30**, but the expected price changed several times during the early stages of the appointment. She alleged that the estimate was revised four times before eventually reaching **$45**, leaving her confused about the actual cost of the service.

The customer said the repeated adjustments made her feel uncomfortable because the service had already begun.

## Leaving Before the Service Was Finished

Rather than continue with the appointment, the customer decided to leave before polish was applied and before the manicure was completed.

According to her review, she believed ending the appointment was the best option after losing confidence in the pricing process.

## A Birthday Disappointment

The customer noted that the appointment had been planned as a birthday treat.

Instead of enjoying a relaxing experience, she said the pricing dispute overshadowed the occasion and left her disappointed. She concluded her review by stating that she intended to visit another salon in the future.

## Editorial Perspective

Clear and consistent pricing is an important part of building customer confidence. When estimates change after a service has already begun, customers may feel uncertain about whether they can make informed decisions.

Open communication before additional charges are applied can help reduce misunderstandings and create a more positive customer experience. Even when pricing adjustments are necessary, explaining the reasons clearly and obtaining customer agreement beforehand can strengthen trust.

## Key Takeaways

- Provide clear estimates before beginning a service.
- Explain any additional charges before performing extra work.
- Transparent communication supports long-term customer trust.
- Respecting customer expectations can improve satisfaction.
- Positive service recovery helps protect business reputation.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this customer review illustrates how pricing transparency and communication can strongly influence customer perception. For beauty businesses, consistent estimates, respectful conversations, and customer-focused service remain essential for building lasting relationships.
