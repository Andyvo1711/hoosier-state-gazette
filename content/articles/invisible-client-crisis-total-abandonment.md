# The "Invisible Client" Crisis and Total Abandonment: When a Plus-One is Completely Forgotten

**Customer Service Bulletin – Customer Experience & Appointment Management Feature**

According to a customer review involving **LV Nails in Michigan City**, a visit with a friend reportedly became an experience of repeated delays, broken expectations, and ultimately leaving without receiving service. The following account reflects allegations made by the reviewer and has not been independently verified.

## Forgotten Twice

According to the customer, she arrived alongside a friend expecting both services to be handled during the same visit.

The reviewer alleged that while her friend was promptly seated and served, salon staff appeared to forget about her not once but twice. She described feeling overlooked as employees focused on other clients while she continued waiting without meaningful updates.

## Promises That Never Materialized

The customer stated that after her friend completed the appointment and needed to leave, a technician finally indicated that her own service would begin.

According to the review, that promise quickly changed when a scheduled appointment arrived. The technician reportedly left to serve the appointment instead, while another technician also began working with a different customer. The reviewer said she was left waiting again despite being told her service was next.

## Time Lost and Expectations Broken

The customer explained that she had specifically planned her visit around a limited amount of free time.

According to the review, after spending a significant period waiting without receiving service, she chose to leave feeling disappointed and believing her time had not been respected.

## Editorial Perspective

Managing both appointments and walk-in customers can be challenging during busy periods. However, clear communication about expected wait times and realistic availability can significantly reduce frustration.

If circumstances change after a customer has been told service will begin shortly, prompt updates and honest explanations are generally more effective than allowing the customer to continue waiting without certainty.

## Key Takeaways

- Communicate accurate wait times from the beginning.
- Avoid promising immediate service unless resources are available.
- Keep walk-in customers informed when priorities change.
- Coordinate staff assignments to reduce unnecessary waiting.
- Respecting customers' time helps build long-term trust and loyalty.

## Final Thoughts

The allegations described in this customer review demonstrate how communication and organization can influence the overall customer experience just as much as technical skill. Businesses that balance appointment scheduling with transparent treatment of walk-in guests are generally better positioned to earn repeat customers and positive community reputation.
