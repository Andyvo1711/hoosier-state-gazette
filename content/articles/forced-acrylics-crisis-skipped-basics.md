# The "Forced Acrylics" Crisis and Skipped Basics: When Gimmicks Cover Up Incompetence

**Customer Service Bulletin – Trend Awareness & Service Protocol Standards Feature**

According to a customer review involving **Top Nails in Lafayette**, what began as a pleasant salon visit with her husband reportedly became disappointing when a requested nail style led to unexpected recommendations and concerns about the quality of the final result.

## A Request for "Milk Nails"

The customer said both she and her husband initially enjoyed their pedicure services.

According to her review, she later requested a popular "milk nails" look. She alleged that the technician told her a full acrylic set was necessary to achieve the desired appearance. Although surprised, she agreed to the suggested upgrade because she trusted the recommendation.

## Expectations for Basic Manicure Care

The customer stated that even after agreeing to acrylics, she expected a standard manicure that included attention to her cuticles.

According to her account, she felt those basic preparation steps were not performed. Watching her husband receive what she believed was a more complete manicure during the same visit increased her disappointment and left her questioning the value of the upgraded service.

## Concerns About the Final Result

The customer also reported that the acrylic nails appeared uneven after they were completed.

According to her review, she believed the technician noticed she was unhappy but continued finishing the service instead of discussing possible adjustments. She later shared comparison photos online to illustrate the difference between the look she wanted and the result she received.

## Editorial Perspective

Beauty trends continue to evolve, and customers often rely on technicians to recommend the most appropriate techniques. Clear explanations about available options, expected results, and pricing can help customers make informed decisions before work begins.

Likewise, consistent attention to foundational manicure procedures and open communication throughout the appointment can strengthen customer confidence, particularly when premium services are involved.

## Key Takeaways

- Explain service options before recommending upgrades.
- Maintain consistent manicure preparation standards.
- Encourage open communication during appointments.
- Address customer concerns before completing the service.
- Quality workmanship and transparency help build long-term trust.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this customer review illustrates how technical execution, communication, and customer expectations can influence overall satisfaction. For beauty businesses, combining professional expertise with transparent recommendations and responsive customer care remains essential for developing lasting client relationships.
