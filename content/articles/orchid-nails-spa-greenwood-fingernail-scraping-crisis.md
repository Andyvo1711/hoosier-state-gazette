# Orchid Nails & Spa in Greenwood: The "Fingernail Scraping" Crisis and Technical Flaws — When Sloppiness Destroys a Set

*Customer Service Bulletin – Technical Standards & Service Quality*

## Removal techniques matter

According to a reported customer experience, the appointment began with concerns during gel polish removal. The reviewer alleged that after difficulty removing the existing gel, the technician attempted to scrape product away using his own fingernails rather than completing the process with appropriate professional techniques.

The customer felt this approach was uncomfortable and raised concerns about both professionalism and potential damage to the natural nail.

## Application quality

The review further states that the replacement gel manicure appeared uneven under normal lighting. The customer also believed several nail edges were not properly sealed, resulting in rough areas that repeatedly caught on hair during daily activities.

Small finishing details often have a significant impact on how customers evaluate the overall quality of a manicure.

## Durability concerns

According to the reviewer, the manicure began chipping within two days, while the accompanying gel pedicure showed similar problems shortly afterward. The customer later updated the original review to include these additional durability concerns, concluding that the issues were not isolated.

## Editorial perspective

Professional nail services depend on careful preparation, appropriate removal methods, consistent product application, and thorough finishing techniques. Customers paying for gel services generally expect both immediate cosmetic quality and lasting durability.

For salons, investing time in proper removal procedures and quality control can strengthen customer confidence and encourage long-term loyalty.
