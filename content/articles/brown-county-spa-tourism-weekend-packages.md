Several spas near Nashville, Indiana, the small artisan town at the heart of Brown County, have introduced weekend getaway packages this year aimed at attracting visitors from Indianapolis seeking a lower-key wellness retreat alternative to busier destinations.

The packages typically bundle overnight accommodations at partner cabins and inns with a full spa treatment menu, positioning Brown County's rolling hills and quieter rural setting as a selling point for visitors seeking a more relaxed atmosphere than a typical weekend shopping trip through the town's galleries and shops.

"We're never going to compete with the shops on the main strip for foot traffic, and we've stopped trying to," said the owner of a Brown County spa that partnered with a nearby cabin rental company on a new weekend package. "What we can offer is genuine quiet and a slower pace, and it turns out there's real demand for that from people who are exhausted by how busy their weekends usually are."

The packages have found a particular audience among visitors from the Indianapolis metro area looking for a shorter drive than more distant wellness retreat destinations, while still offering a meaningful change of scenery from urban and suburban daily life. Spa owners said early bookings have skewed toward couples and small groups of friends rather than solo travelers.

Regional tourism officials have expressed interest in supporting the wellness tourism packages as part of broader efforts to diversify Brown County tourism beyond its traditional reliance on fall foliage season visitors. Spa owners said they plan to expand package offerings into additional months of the year if demand continues to build.
